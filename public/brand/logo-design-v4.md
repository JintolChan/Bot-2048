## 提示词

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a distinctive ARTISTIC TYPOGRAPHIC LOGO for a game named exactly "BOT 2048". The entire logo is made ONLY from these letters and numerals; no separate symbol, mascot or icon.

Art direction: a bespoke compact interlocking lettering sculpture, expressive contemporary arcade identity, playful confidence and graphic-design intelligence. Go well beyond typing a font. Use a custom two-level staggered composition: BOT in an upper cluster and 2048 in a lower cluster, the two clusters tightly nested with a small controlled overlap. Upper BOT is slightly offset to the left; lower 2048 extends a little right, producing an energetic roughly square silhouette. Bold wide block letterforms with beautifully drawn angular-soft hybrid corners, oversized geometric counters and a few intentional stepped cuts inherited from game tiles. The lower part of T can extend into the gap between 0 and 4 below as an elegant interlocking detail, without obscuring any digit. A small number of crisply outlined negative-space separation cuts let letters cross and nest. Sculpt the negative spaces as carefully as the positive forms. Visually unify the whole as one memorable logotype, not two separate lines of ordinary typesetting.

Priorities: name must read BOT then 2048, all seven glyphs recognizable, only ONE B, only ONE 2048. Preserve legibility even with the expressive arrangement. No extra repeated or mirrored letters. Not an extreme heavy-metal logo or graffiti scribble. NO plain horizontal baseline. NO surrounding frame. Avoid rigid 8-bit off-the-shelf fonts; this is custom artistic lettering with a hint of pixel-game DNA.

Colors: upper BOT warm off-white #EBEFED, lower 2048 mint green #84EEB4; small portions of forest green #238866 may articulate just one overlap. Flat fills, no glow, no bevel or 3D, no gradient, no texture, no outline around entire design. Precise clean edges with no speckling.

Canvas: square, single centered finished logo occupying around 72% of width and 65% of height, good breathing space. FULLY OPAQUE uniform dark forest-black #080C0B background; never transparent. No mockups, no concept sheet, no captions or extraneous text. A crafted premium independent-game logo, suitable as a compact brand stamp on a game loading screen.
