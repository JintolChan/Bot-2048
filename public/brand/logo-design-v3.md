# BOT 2048 · Pixel wordmark v3

直接以 BOT 2048 名称设计像素字标，不附加独立图标。统一方格笔画、阶梯转角；BOT 使用米白色，2048 使用薄荷绿。深色背景 PNG。由内置 image_gen 生成，未替换网站素材。

## 提示词

Use case: logo-brand.
Design a new wordmark-only logo for the onchain game named exactly "BOT 2048". The NAME ITSELF IS THE ENTIRE LOGO. No separate icon, no monogram, no emblem, no symbols, no additional B, no tagline, no decorative tiles outside the letters.
Creative direction: refined custom pixel typography, retro arcade meets contemporary minimalist branding. Draw every letter and digit from the SAME precise square pixel grid with consistent thick modular strokes. Bold angular stepped contours, clearly defined rectangular counters, genuinely pixel-built glyphs rather than a normal rounded font. BOT uppercase. All seven glyphs share a cap height and stroke weight. Pay special attention to making B, O, T, 2, 0, 4, 8 immediately readable and visually distinct; B's two counters and 8's two counters must be clean. The single B appears only once as the first letter of BOT.
Subtle distinctive detail: a few carefully placed grid-aligned inset cuts at stroke junctions evoke tiles merging, but all glyphs remain solid readable silhouettes. Precise optical kerning: clear small gaps between each glyph, moderately larger gap between BOT and 2048, no touching letters. Flat minimalist graphic design, professionally balanced wordmark, suitable for a game header and social avatar crop of the whole name.
Text verbatim: "BOT 2048".
Color: BOT warm off-white #EBEFED; 2048 mint green #84EEB4. Keep two flat text colors, no multicolored individual pixels.
Layout: one single horizontal wordmark centered on a landscape 2:1 canvas, generous negative space, wordmark occupying about 80% of width and 28% of height. No other visual elements.
BACKGROUND MUST BE FULLY OPAQUE solid near-black forest #080C0B covering the entire canvas. Do NOT generate transparency or an alpha cutout. Clean solid fills. Absolutely no gradients, glow, shadows, bevels, 3D, outlines, distressed texture, scanlines, glitch noise, white halos or fringes. Crisp professionally drawn pixel typography. Not a mockup or a concept sheet. One finished logo.
