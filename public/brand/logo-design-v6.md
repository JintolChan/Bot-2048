## 提示词

Use case: logo-brand, precise-object-edit.
Redesign the supplied BOT 2048 wordmark according to this specific correction: the colors are approved, but the lettering must become SQUARE-GRID CONSTRUCTED and SEAMLESSLY FUSED. It should look like ONE continuous typographic puzzle, with no sense of separate cut-out characters.

KEEP the exact name "BOT 2048", BOT above 2048, a compact roughly square composition, and the approved palette: top BOT pale cream-sage #D7E7C5; bottom 2 light sage #B8D99B, 0 soft green #8DCEA1, 4 mint #84EEB4, 8 light mint #A5F5B9. All colors flat.

CHANGE the glyph construction radically from the rounded reference:
- All lettering is drawn on ONE consistent coarse SQUARE CELL GRID. Bold square-pixel / modular geometric lettering with orthogonal 90-degree corners. Rectangular strokes, squared counters, stair-step diagonals for 2 and 4. No pill shapes, no curves, no diagonal smooth slopes, no drooping tails.
- FUSE the neighboring glyphs into one joined connected silhouette. B and O share a vertical stroke; O and T meet through a shared top bar. The lower edge of BOT joins directly into the upper strokes of 2048. The digits also physically meet and share selected vertical strokes or horizontal bars.
- CRITICAL: REMOVE THE BLACK GUTTERS / BLACK OUTLINES / SPACING BETWEEN CHARACTERS. Colored areas meet edge-to-edge with absolutely no dark seams. Where green shades differ they simply abut at square grid boundaries. Every part belongs to the same connected solid shape.
- Readability must come from carefully designed square negative-space counters and short open-ended square notches, NOT from outlining and separating the letters. Black is allowed inside the necessary counters and open notches, but never as a continuous separator around a whole character.
- Make BOT and 2048 recognizable as custom ligatures, exact letters and digits in correct order. Do not add or repeat any glyph, do not turn the name into a generic labyrinth. Bespoke memorable typography. Only one B.
- Preserve a unified blocky rectangular silhouette with a few stepped outer edges, all organized on the same square-cell scale. It should feel like 2048 tiles have actually merged into a single branded tile-mosaic.

No independent icon, no board underneath, no seven separate tile badges, no additional text, no frame, no gradients, texture, shadow, 3D, glow or distressed edges.
Single finished logo centered on a square canvas, occupying about 75% of width, with generous margin. FULLY OPAQUE SOLID #080C0B background, absolutely no transparent cutout. Ultra-clean precise vector-style shapes.
